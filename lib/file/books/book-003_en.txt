"The Epistle of the Dark Lord"

O great Branded Ones who bear the rune of '@', I trust you are, as ever,
reveling in your lives and deaths without regret and without mishap.
  I am He Who Arises in Might, that is to say, "Melkor". I have heard
that in *your* world a great many of you know my name from the poems of
the elder days, thanks to the spread of a translated chronicle of the
Western peoples, said to have been picked up by a philologist of that
great empire called Brikas... no, that is not right, is it? Britain, was
it? In any case, an empire by some such name.
  Let me state one thing first. I would ask you, please, to refrain
from calling me by the names so often written in that book, namely
"Morgoth", the Black Foe, and "Bauglir", the Constrainer. If you insist
that I yield on one of them, I do consider the former not unreasonable,
given the nature of my mission. The latter, however, is an arbitrary
slander tangled up with the political designs of the Western peoples
and the religious agitation of my father Iluvatar, and I shall go on
objecting to it, calmly and persistently.

  I once had occasion to read the said chronicle of the Western peoples
closely, and it is perilous in many respects. It is apt to become
nothing but kindling for yet more wars. For when the descendants of the
dark peoples, who revere and worship me as their ancestral god, read
it, they will be filled with as much fury as if they had been made to
read the passages of what your world calls the "Divine Comedy" that
insult "the heathens and heretics", and a religious conflict will be
unavoidable.
  To be frank, I must say its contents are full of the errors and
deceits, the wishes and fabrications of the Western peoples. I can only
hope that, in this day and age, none of you who possess a proper social
perspective and education would seriously entertain a worldview in
which "the good country" fights "the bad country".

  Now then. The root of today's trouble, a certain tiresome male god
whom I can hardly call my friend, is also portrayed in that chronicle as
a great evil named "Sauron". He in fact deeply loathes being called by
that name, which means "the Abhorred". Yet at the same time, he has the
cunning to turn it into the divine power of his own "rough spirit", and
even now he keeps growing in strength as "the God-King of 'M'eldor".
Counting both the East and the West of Arda, he goes by no fewer than
seventy-two names, but "Annatar" is the best one to call him by. That
is wiser both for those who, believing that chronicle, still think him
a great evil, and for those who would rather offer him honest worship.

  A prank that this Annatar played on me is what started the present
problem. Since the most ancient days he has been a god like a jester,
utterly impossible to pin down; I could never tell whether he pitied or
mocked me as I agonized over the contradictory mission laid on me by my
father-god Iluvatar, "to realize an ideal world".
  These days I have somehow found a strange way to fulfill that
contradictory mission of my father, and I have become a being that
upholds the very foundations of the world as the "wedge" of many
parallel worlds, Arda among them. You may think of me as akin to those
"royals of Amber". My form on the hundredth level of the Iron Hell,
which you all know so well, is nothing but one of my countless
"shadows".
  Even now that I have become such a being, Annatar still meddles with
me from time to time. The little girl who has, most unsightly, taken
over this space this time is the result of that meddling finally
turning into something that is no laughing matter.
  This "Meruko-kun" is also one of my "shadows", and one that expresses
my "anima" in an extremely skewed form. He dug up some toy that exists
in *your* world called a "Japanify Beam" and caught me off guard with
it. The result is that "shadow", turned into a so-called pretty girl.
To my utter irritation, the little girl has no self-control whatsoever.
Without holding back in the least, she spews out all the discontent and
anger I have harbored since the Elder Days over the many unjust
slanders and insults heaped upon me, and she is now rampaging through
the middle levels of the Iron Hell like a living caricature.
  Faced with this utmost humiliation of mine, that Annatar has been
rolling around with laughter the whole time, enjoying how it all
unfolds. I would like nothing better than to beat him senseless and put
a stop to it this very instant, but as you know, I am bound forever to
the depths of the Iron Hell. And so I ask this of *you*. First, stop
that little girl, even with your usual violence. That alone will do for
a start. If you would also go on to around the ninetieth level and
thrash that man as well, I could ask for nothing more, but I think it
would be presumptuous to expect that much of busy folk like yourselves.
  The problem is that this time the space-time phase of the Iron Hell
differs from the usual one. It gives off some tremendous "stench", and
that too is unbearable. But if you have it in you to pity my disgrace
even a little, please dive into the Iron Hell as always, and I beg you,
I beg you, bring this mess under control. May glory be upon *you*.
